RECORDING 01

Belief & the blessings of Ramadhan

Maulana Sayyid M. Rabey Hasani Nadwi delivering speech after JUma prayer in Takiya Masjid at Raebareli. He describes the standard of believer and non-believer in the eye of Allah Almighty and the blessings of the month of Ramadhan. 4th of Ramadhan 1432H - 05 August 2011

RECORDING 02

Benefiting from Ramadhan

Maulana Husain Ahmad Gujrati delivered this speech in the mosque of Takiya Kalan, Raebareli on 11th of Ramadhan 1432H emphasizing on the significance of Ramadhan and benefiting from it. 12 August 2011

RECORDING 03

Ramadhan essentials & preparation

Maulana Sayyid Bilal Abdul Hai Hasani Nadwi delivers speech after juma prayer on the topic of Ramadhan Essentials, preparation and precautions. 18 Ramadhan 1432H - 19 Agust 2011
Watch on YouTube ↗Back to recordings ↑

RECORDING 04

Respect for Ramadhan & making the most of it

Maulana Sayyid Abdullah Hasani Nadwi delivers speech on Ramadhan and respect & obedience regarding it, and how to utilize completely the month of Ramadhan and stay at takiya kalan, the place of many saints of Allah. 25 Ramadhan 1432H - 26 August 2011
