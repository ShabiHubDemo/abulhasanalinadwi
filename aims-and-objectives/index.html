BACKGROUND & CONTEXT

About the centre

Dar-e-Arafat & its educational work
Dar-e-Arafat is an old institution involved in religious, dawah, training and social welfare activities. It was established 35 years ago by Maulana Mohammad Rabey Hasani Nadwi and Maulana Sayyid Muhammad Al-Hasani under the patronage of Hazrat Maulana Abul Hasan Ali Hasani Nadwi. During 35 years period it has performed a number of educational and welfare tasks. A training course is continuing for the students (boys) qualifying from Darul Uloom Nadwatul Ulama. Moreover, lectures from people of Dawah, learned men, scholars, intellectuals are also being organized.
Sayyid Ahmad Shaheed Academy & publications
In order to activate its department of publications, a department was established eight years before by the name of Syed Ahmad Shaheed Academy, which, with all thanks to Allah, the Almighty, has printed about 40 important books in Urdu, Arabic and English languages.
The centre & its building
Since Dar-e-Arafat is the representative institution of Hazrat Maulana Abul Hasan Ali Hasani Nadwi’s ideology and dawah, an extreme urgency was felt after the demise of Hazrat Maulana to establish a permanent department to clarify his thinking and its practical structure which can effectively carryout the tasks of Dar-e-Arafat in Dawah, ideological and research fields. This department has been established by the name of “Imam Abul Hasan Ali Alnadwi center for Research, Dawah and Islamic thoughts”. With all thanks to Allah, its magnificent building has also been completed with the help of some philanthropists. It consists of two floor library, a big hall and 14 rooms. One big room is for lectures, another room for historical documents has been properly arranged relating to Hazrat Maulana. Four rooms are used for official purposes; one room is being used for computer and another for composing work and one room is used as guest room and four rooms are used for research workers.
Inauguration · 29–30 March 2008
It was formally inaugurated on 29-30 March 2008, which was participated by important people throughout the country and an important delegation from Iran also took part in it and Dr. Syed Salman Nadwi came from South Africa. On this occasion an important seminar was held on the Dawah and thinking of Maulana Syed Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi and about 30 treatises were presented. Since then this center is busy in its work.
Magazines & publications
From 6 January 2009, a monthly magazine is being published in Urdu under the name of Payam-e-Arafat whose English and Hindi edition entitled “Arafat Voice” and “Arafat Kiran” respectively, is being published regularly, by the grace of Allah. However, a magazine by the name of Tameer-e-Afkar (quarterly in Urdu) is also being published, from past three years.
Primary schools & welfare
A programme of establishing primary Islamic schools is also going on in the neighbouring places. Up to now, about eleven primary schools have been established, by the grace of Allah.The current expenditure of the above is about eighty thousands per month on the salaries of staff and primary school teachers, as well as on the research workers.
Research, training & support team
  • Four research workers are in the Department of Research and Study. One is working on Qura’an, one on Hadith, one on Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh) and one on Islamic thinking.
  • In the department of training, there are 11 research workers.
  • One librarian with one assistant and two helpers.
  • One is in charge of computer center who provides training in computer and performs projects for the center.
