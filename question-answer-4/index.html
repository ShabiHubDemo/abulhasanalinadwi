    Miscellaneous Questions

    Q)Salam 1.farz namaz mei shuru ki 2 raqat bhari or baki khali q rhti hn? 2.isha namaz mei vitier namaz mei gr dua-e-qunoot gar yaad na ho to kya uski jagah kuch or padha ja sakta hy? suna hy ki uski jagah 3baar surah ikhlas padhi ja sakti hy? 3. kya Huzur Pak(S.a.w) k chahcha Hzrat abu talib imaan laye they? 4.hadees mei kuch aayat ya koi bhi amal namaz baad k liye subah shaam ka hota hy for ex 10 martaba durood-shareef payare nabi ki shafa't hasil krne k liye subah shaam padhe ya surah hshr ki 3 aytein pahei subah-sham.. to mera sawal hy ki yahanpe sham ki murad kab se hy?? kya asr baad ya phir maghrib ya isha baad??
    A)(1) Bukhari ke Hadith no. 770 and 776 me hai ki Allah ke Rasool (S.A.W.) ka yahi tareeqa tha. (2)Agar Dua-e-Qunoot yad na ho to, us ki jagah yeh dua padhe: "Rabbana Aatina Fid Duniya Hasanatan wa fil aakhirati hasanatan wa qina aazaban naar" agar yeh bhi yaad na ho to dua-e-qunoot yaad hone tak yeh dua padhe: "Allahumma-gh-firli" teen baar, ya phir yeh padhe: "ya rabbi" teen baar. (Raddul Mukhtaar: 2/443) Dua-e-Qunoot ke badle surah ikhlaas padhna kahin bhi sabit nahi hai. (fatawa darul uloom deoband:4/158) (3)woh eimaan nahi laye the. (4) Maghrib baad Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Shalwar ka takhne se nicha hona

    Q)Assalam alaikum, mera sawal ye hai ke payjama ka takhne se nicha pehanna kaisa hai? kya ye haram hai ya makrooh tehreemi hai? please daleel ke sath jald se jald explain kre...
    A)Ghamad me paijama ko takhnon ke neeche karna makrooh-tehreemi hai, Bukhari Shareef me hai ke jo shakhs apne Izaar (Pijama, wagairah) ko ghamad ki wajah se takhnon ke neeche latkayega to Allah uski taraf nazar karam bhi na farmayega.(Bukhari: Hadith No. 5783) Aur agar aisi niyat nahi hai to makrooh tanzeehi hai, beherhaal, ehtiyaat behtar hai. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Movie me kisi nabi ya sahaba ko dikhna

    Q)Assalam alaikum, mera sawal ye hai ke aajkal kuch islamic history per movie ban rhi hai jisme sahaba ko dikhaya gya hai, kya aisi movie dekhna jaez hai? aur kya agar kisi movie me kisi nabi (yusuf a.s, moosa a.s, etc) ko dikhya jae to uska dikhna jaez hoga? plz give me answer with daleel because some knowlegable person says that it is jaez.
    A)Movie me SAHABAH aur AMBIYA ki jo tasweer pesh ki jati hai woh unke murtabe aur taqaddus se bahut ghatiya hai, actor bhi kafir, fasiq aur fajir hote hain. Is tarah ki Movie se unka status neecha hota hai, aur hum usko dekh kar gunehgaar hote hain, aur ye AZMAT-E-SAHABAH ke khilaaf hai, is liye EMAANI taqaza hai ki aesi cheezon se mukammal ijtinaab kiya jaye. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Sharyi masla

    Q)Assalamu alykum warehmatullahi wabarkatu..... mera sawal yahai ke agar kisi ladki ki lash nadi me mili ho aur uske paas koyi aysi chez nahi hai jis ke zarye ye pata chle ke ye hindu hai ya musalman our aas paas ke loog bhi usko nahi jante our kisi bhi tariqe se ye pata nahi chala ke wo hindu hai ya musalman ..to isko ab dafnaya jaye ga ya jalaya jayega ..meherbani farma kar is masle ka sharye hukum batayen.,, [khuda hafiz]
    A)Asalamualaikum wa Rahmatullah wa Barkatuhu Answer: Agar libaas wagairah se musalmaan ya gair-musalmaan hone ka andaza ho jaye, tab to is ke mutabiq amal kiya jaye, matlab agar musalman ki alamat ho to gusul de kar namaz bhi padhi jaye aur agar kafir ki alamat ho to is ko bagair namaz ke dafan kar diya jaye. Agar kisi tarah bhi pata na chal pa raha hai ke musalman ya gair musalman hai to aisi surat me dusre area se andaza kiya jaye ga. jahan laash dastiyaab hoyi hai, agar is ilaqa me musalman area ho to musalman mana jaye ga, aur agar gair-muslim area hai to gair-muslim, jo cheez ziyada qareeb ho isi ke mutabiq amal kiya jayega. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Hijab

    Q)Salam mera ek sawal aur hai ki 1.Islam mei aoratoan(ladies) ko baloan(hair)k liye khaas hijab karna kyoon lazim hai?? iske peechey kya logic hai ki baaloan(hair) ko dhaankna kyun zaroori hai?
    A)Asalamualaikum wa Rahmatullah wa Barkatuhu Jis tarah aurton ke jism ke dusre parts satar me shamil hain, isi tarah unke balon ke bhi parda zaroori hai, baal aurat ki khoobsurti aur us ke Husn me dakhil hai, aur jis tarah uske jism ke dusre parts attractive hote hain aur unke taraf attraction hota hai isi tarah unke balon ka masla bhi hai. is liye unka hijaab zaroori hai. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center
