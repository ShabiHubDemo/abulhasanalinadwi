    Tasawuf

    Q)Who is Mawlana AbdusSubhaan Nakhuda Nadwi Bhatkali? He is teaching in nadwa or devbandh or raibareli?
    A)Asalamualaikum wa Rahmatullah wa Barkatuhu Maulana Abdus Subhaan Nadwi is a person of balanced thinking and deep knowledge. He is a great speaker and writer of high profile. He is among the responsible persons in Madrasa Ziaul Uloom affiliation of Nadwatul Ulama and a successful teacher. He has completed his studies from Jamia Islamia Bhatkal, then Nadwatul Ulama and spent four years of studies in Jamia Islamia Madinah Munawwarah. Thereafter, he is attached to Madrasa Ziaul Uloom and at present he is also head of Department of Tafseer in Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Q)My sister met with an accident on 31 december.please tell me some dua or wazaif.she is in very critical condition.please do remember her in your duas also.
    A)Ghamad me paijama ko takhnon ke neeche karna makrooh-tehreemi hai, Bukhari Shareef me hai ke jo shakhs apne Izaar (Pijama, wagairah) ko ghamad ki wajah se takhnon ke neeche latkayega to Allah uski taraf nazar karam bhi na farmayega.(Bukhari: Hadith No. 5783) Aur agar aisi niyat nahi hai to makrooh tanzeehi hai, beherhaal, ehtiyaat behtar hai. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi CenterAsalmaualaikum wa Rahmatullah Din men 100 martaba yah padhen: “INNA LILLAHI WA INNA ILEHI RAJIW’OON.” Yah padhte waqt iski Meaning par dhiyan den. Meaning: “Hum sab Allah ke liye hain aur hum sabko usi ki paas laut ke jaana hai.” Shuru aur Akhir men 3-3 bar Darood Shareef Padhen. Aur sab ko jama karke ek sitting me 125,000 times "Ya Salamu" Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Q)Assalamu Alaikum. Allah aap hazraat ko jazai e khair ataa farmaye. Mein ye janna chahta hun k kya hamara gair muqallideen k sath sirf fiqhi ikhtilaaf hai ya aqaid mein b hai? Agar hai to kya kya hai? Barai e karam jawab jaldi marhamat farmaye. Wassalaam
    A)Asalamualaikum wa Rahmatullah wa Barkatuhu Hamara Gair muqalideen se ikhtilaaf sirf fiqhi aur nazaryati hai, albatta woh is me itni shiddat aur gulu ikhtiyaar karte hain ke mamla bigarhta chala jata hai. unki shiddat pasandi ki wajah se muslim muasharah me inteshaar ke surat paida hoti hai, is liye unse ulajhne ke bajae hikmat se kaam lena chahiye. ehle hadith hazraat ke nazariyaat aur fiqhi ekhtelafaat ko tafseel me samajhne ke liye is kitaab ka mutala`a bahut mufeed hai: "Maslaki ekhtelafaat aur rah-e-etedaal" written by Mufti Rashid Husain Nadwi (Principal Ziaul Uloom, Raebareli) Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Q)Kya ham molana kaleem siddiqi (phulatwale)ki tehrik me shamil ho sakte he?molana konse maslak se taalluk rakhte he?kya un par akabir ka etemad hasil he?mery rahnumayi farmaye. me unke risale ko padhta hu is liye me puch raha hu.
    A)Kaleem Siddiqui Sb. ki tehreek me shamil hone se ihtiyaat karen. Maslak unka ehle-deoband ka hai. Lekin Apne kaam ko faroogh dene ke liye woh ghulu se kaam lete hain, aur baat saaf saaf jhoot tak pahunch jati hai, is liye ab akabireen unki taeed me ehtiyaat karte hain. Unke risaale main "Naseem-e-hidayat ke jhonke" ke naam se jo column hain woh qabil-e-etebaar nahi, is me muta`adid waqi`aat galat sabit ho chuke hain. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Fazaail e amal

    Q)Assalamualaikum please answer my following questions 1)my friends argue with me about fazaail e amal that it contains zaeef hadith,so how should i answer them about it? 2)what is the difference in aqeedah between us and jamaat e islami? 3)my friends argue that spending 40 days in jamaat is contrary to sunnah?is it true?
    A)(1) Zaeef hadith does not mean that it should be thrown into dustpit, perhaps, for Ahkamat, if there are Sahih Hadith then Zaeef Hadith are not used in practice, and Ulama have always been united on the fact that, for Fazael, Zaeef hadith can be used. However, Zaeef is a phraseology. And sometimes a hadith is Zaeef near some people and sahih near others. (2) The basic difference between our Aqaid and Jamaat-e-Islamia is that they do not prefer anyone above critics, even they have criticized Sahabah Keraam and at places they have even criticized prophets. Whereas, our Aqeedah is that all Prophets are innocent and Sahabah Keraam are ideal people and they are above all critics and criticizing them is completely forbidden. (3) Fixation of 40 days is just for tarbiyat (training) and it is a administrative work. Acourse of 40 days is fixed for Islah-e-Dil (correction of heart) because in forty days man's nature is transformed. This fixation of 40 days is not a Shariah problem that it may be seen in the light of sunnah. It's main moto is to transform the nature of man into religious mode and sometimes this could be achieved in couple of days and sometimes it could not achieved in years. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center Dar-e-Arafat, Takiya Kalan, Raebareli (U.P.)229001

    Female teachers

    Q)What do the Ulama say regarding Muslim girls leaving their homes and going to Schools where there is no proper Hijaab System? Male Teachers teach the female students and vice versa.
    A)Ulama have always believed in necessity of achieving knowledge, thereby, girls can go out of their houses and achieve it, but parda is compulsory and used be taken care of. Because in Quran, it says: "O Prophet! Tell thy wives and daughters, and the believing women, that they should cast their outer garments over their persons (when abroad): that is most convenient, that they should be known (as such) and not molested. And Allah is Oft- Forgiving, Most Merciful." (Ahzaab:59) Therefore, going out of their houses without veil is forbidden and achieving knowledge where there is not system of parda is also not permissible. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center Dar-e-Arafat, Takiya Kalan, Raebareli (U.P.)229001
