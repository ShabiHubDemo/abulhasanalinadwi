Surah Nahl · Ruku 15: commentary

Hazrat Maulana Sayyid M. Rabey Hasani Nadwi delivering commentary of Holy Qur'an of Chapter Nahl (Honey) Para 14, Ruku 15. He described the back-draws of Idolatry and Shirk, distribution of human standards and its base, inequality of man and woman and difference of human and animals. 3rd Ramadhan 1432 H, Takiya Kalan, Raebareli. 05 August 2011
