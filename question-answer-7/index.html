    Sadqa

    Q)Salam Sir Main ne suna hai dukhoon aur museebtoon se bachne ke lye sadqa karna chahye. Mera sawal yeh hai keh kia sadqka ki raqam masjid mein di ja sakti hai? please mujhe answer zaroor dijye ga . Insan jab koi bura khwab dekhta hai ya us ke dil mein bure khyalat atey hain to kia museebtoon dukhoon aur preshanion ke liye sadqa ki raqam masjid mein di ja sakti hai. Jo masjid ke bahir chanda wala box hota hai kia yeh rakam us mein dal dein?
    A)Ahadeeth me muta`adid jagah aya hai ki parehani aur musibat ke waqt sadqa ki madad len, kyunki sadqa har pareshaani aur musibat ko door kar deti hai. Aap ke sawal ke mutabiq jawab yeh hai ki, Sadqa ki raqam masjid me nahi lagai ja sakti hai, yeh gareebon ka haq hota hai. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Centern in low voice at such places because recitation in low voice does not contain any danger of impudence, and recitation in loud voice is Makrooh. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center Dar-e-Arafat, Takiya Kalan, Raebareli (U.P.)229001

    Hajj E Badal

    Q)Salam o alaykum kya Hajj e badal sirf wahi insaan kar sakta hai jo apna hajj kar chuka hai?? ya koi aur bhi kar sakta hai??jaise beta ya beti jiska apna hajj nahi hua hai lekin uska irada apna haj karne ka hai. baraye meharbani iska jawab mujhe Quran aur hadees ki roshni mei dei ki Quran mei kahan kis surah mei iska ziqr hai aur kiss hadees mein iska khualsa hai. mera doosra sawal hai ki aulaad apne parents ya apne doosre rishtoan k liye aisal e sawab ki niyat se paisa,samaan ya khana waghaira nikalte ya dete hain to iske liye kya unke naam se sadqe ki niyat ka paisa us aisal e sawwb k kaam mei lgaya ja sakta hai??ya sadqe ka paisa alaga aur aesal e sawab k liye alag se paisa lagaya jana chahiye?? mera 3rd question ki masjid ki tameer ya uske kaamoan k liye hum kya-kya niyat kar k piasa de sakte hain?for ex:- jaise him ZAQAAT ka paisa masjid ki tameer k liye nahi de sakte.. tou kya sadqe ka paisa..ya sirf aesal e sawaab ki niyat k liye hi de sakte hain?? aapke jawab ka muntazir ali khan mere in emails pe jabad dene ki meharbani karein alikhan76@rediffmail.com just4u800@yahoo.com
    A)(1) Jis Shaks par Hajj farz ho usko chahiye ko woh khud Hajj kar le, aur Hajj-e-Badal me bhi Sahih Tareeqa ye hai ke jo Hajj kar chuka ho as se Hajj karwaya jaye. Jis par Hajj Farz ho chuka ho agar woh Hajj-e-Badal kare tab bhi Ahnaaf ke Yahan Hajj-e-Badal Karahat ke sath Durust ho jayega. Hadith: "Qabila Banu Khas`am me se ek Khatoon Rasoolullah (S.A.W.) ke paas aayee, Kaha Ye Rasoolullah (S.A.W.)! Mere walid par Hajj Farz ho chuka hai aur woh bahut budhe hain, Hajj karne ki Taqat nahi rakhte hain, to kiya main un ki taraf se hajj-e-badal kar sakti hun. Rasoolullah (S.A.W.) ne irshaad farmaya: aap unki taraf se hajj kar sakti hain". (Tirmizi:928) (2) Sadqa ke paise ko Esaal-e-Sawab me lagaya ja sakta hai, sadqa aur esaal-e-sawab dono alag alag nahi hain, saman, khana, etc. sab kuch sadqa kar sakte hain, is ke liye kisi makhsoos tareeqa ko ikhtiyaar karna durust nahi hai. Esaal-e-sawab ka sabse behtar tareeqa sadqa hai, kyunki sadqa se esaal-e-sawab ke durust hone par ehle Sunnah wal Jama`at ka Ittefaaq hai (3) Masjid ki Tameer me jo bhi kaam karwana chahen pehle niyat karle. Esaal-e-sawab ka sabse behtar tareeqa ye hai ki Marhoom ke naam par "SADQA JARIYA" kiya jaye. For Ex. Masjid ya Madrasa tameer karwa dena, Pani wagairah na kahen Nazm kar dena, etc. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Zakat

    Q)Asslamualikum wm wb Dear Sir, I would like to ask regarding "Zakat".I just started working 1year back as a medical Dr. Am I obliged to contribute Zakat? If so,I would like to know how to calculate and upon what kind of properties to calculate. I have a car which I bought in loan scheme where I have to pay specific amount to the bank every month. Kindly let me know that is it necessary to contribute Zakat upon that car and how to calculate if eligible. Moreover,I would like to know that Zakat can be contributed any time or only during the month of Ramadan. Thank you and looking forward to your reply soon. Yours Sincerely, Twahir
    A)Zakaat is obligatory after completing one full year, and it is obligatory on the person who is the owner of 612.36 grams of silver or 87.48 grams of gold or balance of rupees as much as silver or gold or combined, then he will be entitled to pay Zakaat. The year will be calculated according to lunar eclipse (Arabic calendar) rather than solar eclipse (english calendar). Ramadhan is not necessary to pay Zakaat. Car for personal use is not included in the materials entitled for Zakaat. Zakaat is obligatory on the person who is mentally healthy and attained the age of puberty; entitled for paying zakaat; passing one complete year on the materials entitled for paying zakaat; materials entitled for paying zakaat should be free from loan or debts. Materials which are for personal use are not entitled for paying zakaat, for example: residential house, personal clothes, modes of transportation like car, equipments of factory or for farming and domestic furniture. Except ornaments. Wassalam Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi Center

    Sajda shuker

    Q)Sajda shuker kai lia wazu zarori hai kia nahi aur qibla ru ho na bhi sharat hai kia?
    A)Sajda-e-Shukr ke liye Taharat zaroori hai yani ghusul kiye hue ho, aur ba wazu ho, isi tarah qibla ru hona bhi zaroori hai. (Reference: Hindiya-1/136; Kitabul Fatawa-2/451,459; Shami-1/577)
